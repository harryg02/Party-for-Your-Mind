Sample course reading

Accessible course materials give students more ways to learn. A document might be read visually, enlarged, printed, converted to plain text, or presented with customized colors.

Alternate formats do not change the ideas in a document. They change how those ideas are presented so readers can choose a format that works for them.

Listening can support reading

Text-to-speech technology converts written words into spoken audio. It can help people review information, reduce visual fatigue, check their writing, or learn while completing another activity.

A screen reader does more than read text. It also communicates headings, links, buttons, form controls, and other page structure so a person can navigate without seeing the screen.

Compare the difference

Higher contrast can make text and interface controls easier to distinguish from their backgrounds. Contrast settings may be especially useful in bright environments or when a display has limited brightness.

A comfortable color combination is personal. Operating systems and browsers may offer dark mode, contrast themes, color filters, and other display settings.
